## 🧠 About me

> *Turning data into decisions, and ideas into intelligent systems.*

Hi! I'm **Juan** (he/him), a Data Scientist and AI Engineer with a **B.S. in Data Science from Pontificia Universidad Javeriana** (Bogotá, Colombia). I love understanding a problem deeply before solving it with data.

- 🚀 **Now:** Freelance Data Engineer at **Imuko**, building data pipelines for clients (currently Alteon Insurance).
- 👁️ **Before:** MLOps Engineer & **Computer Vision Lead** at Vigías de Colombia — real-time video intelligence on NVIDIA DeepStream with a hybrid YOLO · D-FINE · RT-DETRv2 · PaddlePaddle ensemble.
- 🗄️ **Also:** Database Administrator (Fiduprevisora–FOMAG, Comercializadora TS) — schemas, normalization, ETL, PostgreSQL tuning.
- 🎓 **Thesis:** Explainable AI for Reinforcement Learning — model distillation & neural-activation analysis on GPT-2 Medium.
- 🏆 Platanus Hack 2026 · CS50 · UN Datathon 2024 · H4CKFOREDU (Universidad de Salamanca) · Academic Excellence Scholarship (highest GPA, Data Science).
- 🌱 **Learning:** LLM interpretability, multi-agent orchestration, zero-trust architectures.
- 🗣️ Spanish (native) · English (C1)
